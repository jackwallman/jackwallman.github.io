JACK WALLMAN

RATTLER FEST 2026 · YODELLING COMPETITION WINNER

This year, before the rug gets pulled on cheap AI, I'm going to take the opportiunity to create more. This website is the window onto those creations. Games, apps and of course the site itself.

Once upon a time, if you wanted a space on the web you made yourself one. When I was 12 I had a small corner on my dad's site for a racehorse called Ice Cracker. It was great, anything was possible. Since then the dark shadow of social media has cast over the web and we've been straight-jacketed into profiles and feeds.

Idiots can build anything now. And I'm one of them.
